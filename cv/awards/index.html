Awards, Activities & Commendations

The expanded detail behind the “Awards, Activities & Commendations” line of my CV.

Hackathons

  • Hackathon Name (Year) (GitHub) — what you built and how it placed.
  • Another Hackathon — a one-line summary of your project and result.

Conferences & Events

  • Conference Name — attended or presented; add a link to slides or a write-up.

University

  • Dean’s List / Scholarship — awarded for academic performance in a given year.
  • Prize Name — short note on what it recognised.

Teaching

  • Teaching assistant / tutor — subjects, level and number of students.

Certifications

  • Certification Name — issuer, level and year.
  • Language Certificate — e.g. an exam level and score.
